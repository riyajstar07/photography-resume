About me

I'm Riya, a Delhi-based photographer, videographer and content creator. I love sports, travelling to the mountains and capturing everyday moments the way I see them.

I am also an engineer, and I work as a freelance photographer, videographer and editor. I have covered 50+ events, managed 10+ social media pages and worked with 4+ cafés.

My Instagram has crossed 1M+ reach, and I continue to create for Instagram and YouTube.

Outside work, I ride motorcycles, have played several sports at district level, and enjoy learning new skills and taking on adventurous experiences.
